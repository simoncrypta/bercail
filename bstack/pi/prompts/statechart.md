---
description: Model a stateful change in TLA+, check it with tla-ts, chart it for the owner, and replay it on the real code.
---
Load the `statechart` skill and follow it. The target: $ARGUMENTS

You have no `statechart` skill when `[bstack] statechart` is off. Then tell the person to set `statechart = true` under `[bstack]` in `~/.config/bercail/config.toml` and run `bercail reconfigure` (it installs tla-ts with npm), and stop.
